SHEIN 可酌情协助处理订单的发货、配送、退货、退款或付款问题，并会以合理方式处理客服咨询。除非 SHEIN 政策另有明确规定，任何此类协助均属好意，不构成 SHEIN 解决由承运商、银行、支付处理方、第三方卖家或其他第三方造成之问题的合同义务。若需第三方介入才能解决，我们可能会将您转介给该第三方。

原文 · 英文

SHEIN may, in its discretion, assist with questions about the shipment, delivery, return, refund, or payment of an order, and will handle customer service inquiries in a reasonable manner. Unless a SHEIN policy expressly states otherwise, any such assistance is a courtesy and does not create a contractual obligation for SHEIN to resolve issues caused by carriers, banks, payment processors, Third-Party Sellers, or other third parties. Where a third party's involvement is necessary to resolve your issue, we may refer you to that third party.
