Temu has handed Missourians a false bill of goods wrapped in discounts and delivered with deception. While consumers were promised savings, the app was quietly harvesting personal data and hiding the truth. We’ve filed a lawsuit against Temu because Missourians deserve honesty, not hidden schemes.
